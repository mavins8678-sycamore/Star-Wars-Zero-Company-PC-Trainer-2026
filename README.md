## 🎮 About the Game

STAR WARS Zero Company is a single-player, turn-based tactics game released on August 27, 2026. It was developed by Bit Reactor in collaboration with Respawn Entertainment and Lucasfilm Games, and published by Electronic Arts. Set during the twilight of the Clone Wars, it follows former Republic officer Hawks and an unconventional squad of Operators.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **STAR WARS Zero Company™**. This trainer enhances the tactical campaign by supporting Operators during turn-based missions and managing resources at The Den. Options focus on health, Action Points, Advantage, credits, Intel, influence, and mission progression while preserving the game's squad-based combat, character customization, operations, and optional permadeath systems.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Combat takes place in turns. Each Operator normally has three Action Points that can be spent on movement, positioning, and other actions. Select an Operator, choose a highlighted destination, and use the unlimited Action Points option when you want to reposition freely or reach cover without ending the turn early.

### Tools & Inventory

At The Den, the campaign hub, you can recruit and progress Operators, customize their appearances and specializations, purchase equipment, and manage loadouts. Unlimited Credits makes it easier to test different equipment choices without waiting for additional campaign income.

### World & Environment

Hawks selects available tactical missions and non-combat operations from the Holotable. Missions advance the campaign and can move the squad into a new cycle, while operations consume Intel and can reward items, credits, and influence. Complete conversations and squad activities at The Den to develop bonds and unlock personal missions and support abilities.

### Combat & Weapons

During a mission, Operators can move, attack with ranged or melee weapons, call for backup, enter Overwatch, rally an incapacitated teammate, and use special actions powered by Advantage. The trainer's health, Action Point, Advantage, injury, and permadeath options are intended for controlled experimentation with cover, squad synergies, mission objectives, and different tactical approaches.
